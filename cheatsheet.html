K-WASUB · cheat sheet

Modern bass synth for the M-VAVE FM-1 · firmware 1.1 BETA · HOLD = keep the button pressed

The basics

SELECT knobchoose a row
ALGO knobchange the row chosen
K1–K4the page's first four settings (written at the bottom of the screen)
PRESETS knobon HOME and the sound lists: the sounds · elsewhere: the page's views
a page buttonopens its first view · again: the next view
OCT− / OCT+octave down / up

Holds

PLAYHOLDHOLD: the keys stay on
SELHOLDCOMPARE with the saved sound
FXHOLDFX PADS: the keys play effects
HOMEHOLDCOMBINE A + B (the two oscillators)
GLOHOLDCLASSIC ⇄ SHIFT mode
RECHOLD stoppedPANIC: all notes off
RECHOLD playing+ a knob: record AUTOMATION · alone: erase it
SAVEHOLDon SAVE: WRITE · in a list: favourite · elsewhere: + OCT− UNDO, OCT+ REDO
OCT−/OCT+HOLDUNDO / REDO (with a knob turned: SHIFT columns)
EDIT SEQHOLDRANDOM: shakes the view shown

Shortcuts

OCT− + OCT+PANIC, any time (notes, pads, echoes off)
LFOHOLD + knobQUICK ASSIGN: LFO 2 on that setting (+OCT− LFO 1, +OCT+ LFO 3)
ENVHOLD + knobthe MOD envelope on that setting
LFOHOLD + white 1–8WOBBLE TAP: 1/2 1/4 1/8 1/16 1/32 1/4T 1/8T 1/16T, on the beat
PERFORM: OCT+HOLDthe keys are FX PADS
REC (other views)TAP tempo
MIDICC 1 wheel · CC 74 cutoff · CC 71 resonance · CC 123 notes off · any CC with MIDI LEARN

SEQ BASS

PLAYstart / stop the bass and drums
white keysthe 16 steps of the page · tap: on / off
white keyHOLD +K1 note · K2 length · K3 condition · K4 velocity
OCT−/OCT+page of 16 steps (32 / 64 steps)
RECstopped: keys write notes step by step · playing: record live
SEQ againPIANO ROLL: keys write the notes, SAVE names the pattern
rowsPRESET · LENGTH (64 … 8, 7 … 1) · COPY / PASTE · CHAIN · SEQUENCE: keys play or transpose

DRUMS

black keys 1–8choose the instrument and play it
black 9 / 10mute the track / clear it
white keysits steps on the page · held + K1–K4: locks
OCT−HOLD + blackmute / unmute that instrument
OCT−/OCT+the page of 16 steps
kits808 · 909 · dub techno · acoustic · KICK DRIVE · SNARE SNAPPY

FX PADS (FXHOLD)

white 1–8wobble speed
white 9–16wobble depth, shape
black 1–4stutter (held)
black 5–11tape stop · DJ low · DJ high · pump · saturate · FREEZE · crush
K1–K4cutoff · resonance · filter type · morph A/B
ALGOQUANTIZE: a pad waits for the bar, 1/2 … 1/64

Sounds & saving

SAVE ×1SAVE page · the keys type the name
SAVE ×2 / ×3SELECT PATCH A (factory) / B (yours)
SAVEHOLD on SAVEWRITE the sound
SEL on SAVELOAD the slot chosen with K1
lists: K4CAT: ALL · SUB · REESE · GROWL · WOBBLE · PLUCK · 808 · DRONE
SAVE: K4 twiceleft: NEW PRESET · right: NEW SONG
